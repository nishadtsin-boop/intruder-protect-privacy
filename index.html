Privacy Policy – Intruder Protect

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Developer: Nishad (Kochi, Kerala, India)

Intruder Protect ("the App") helps you protect your phone from unauthorized use. You register the faces of people you trust. If someone whose face is not trusted opens the apps you choose to protect, or looks at your screen from behind you, the App takes a photo with the front camera so you can see who it was.

This policy explains what data the App uses, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Face data and intruder photos are processed and stored only on your device.
  • We do not upload, sell, or share your face data or photos with anyone.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect limited device information as described in section 5.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data it has stored.

2. Data the App uses

DataWhy it is usedWhere it is stored
Trusted face data (a numeric face template created from photos you register) To recognize you and the people you trust, so they are not treated as intruders. On your device only, in the App's private storage. It is never uploaded.
Intruder photos (front-camera photos with the date and time) Taken only when an untrusted face opens a protected app or is detected looking at the screen from behind, so that you can review them. On your device only, in the App's private storage. You can view and delete them at any time.
App usage events (which app is in the foreground) To know when one of the apps you chose to protect has been opened. Processed on the device in real time and not sent anywhere.

Face detection and recognition run entirely on your device. Camera frames are analyzed in memory and discarded immediately, unless an intruder photo is saved as described above.

3. Permissions

  • Camera – to register trusted faces, detect who is using the phone, and take intruder photos.
  • Usage access – to detect when a protected app is opened.
  • Accessibility Service – see "Use of the Accessibility Service API" below.
  • Foreground service / notifications – to keep protection running and to alert you when an intruder photo is taken.
  • Internet – used only to load ads (section 5). Your face data and photos are never sent over the internet.

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings. Some features will stop working if you do.

Use of the Accessibility Service API

The App uses Android's Accessibility Service API, but only with your explicit permission. The App asks you to enable it only after showing a disclosure screen that explains how it is used, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

What it is used for: to detect, in real time, when one of the apps you chose to protect comes to the foreground or the screen is unlocked, so the App can check with the front camera whether the person using the phone is a trusted face.

What it does NOT do:

  • It does not read, collect, or store screen content, text you type, passwords, messages, or notifications.
  • It does not perform actions on your behalf, change settings, or interact with other apps.
  • It does not send any data off your device. Only the package name of the app in the foreground is checked, in memory, and it is discarded immediately.

The App is not an accessibility tool for users with disabilities, and the Accessibility Service API is used only for the security feature described above.

4. How we use data

Data is used only to provide the App's features described above. We do not use it for profiling, advertising targeting, or any other purpose, and we have no access to your face data or photos.

5. Third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect and process the following data under Google's own privacy policy:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app interactions with ads)

Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. You can see how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

AdMob never receives your face data or intruder photos.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except for the advertising data that Google AdMob collects as described in section 5, or where disclosure is required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • You can delete individual intruder photos or remove trusted faces from inside the App.
  • Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all trusted face data and intruder photos.

8. Security

All data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access under Android's security model. Because nothing is sent to a server, there is no server-side copy that could be breached.

9. Responsible use

Intruder Protect is meant to protect your own device. Do not install it on another person's device or use it to monitor anyone without their knowledge and consent. You are responsible for following the privacy laws that apply where you live.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Your rights

Because your face data and photos are stored only on your device, you have full control over them and can view, export, or delete them at any time. For any privacy request or question, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
